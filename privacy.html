隱私權政策

CS Graduate Exam Planner・生效日期 2026 年 11 月 8 日

CS Graduate Exam Planner（以下稱「本網站」，網址 https://jimmm-lab.github.io/cs-grad-exam-plan/）是個人製作、非營利的研究所考試讀書計畫與教材網站。這份政策說明本網站收集哪些資料、怎麼使用，以及你可以怎麼刪除。

1. 收集哪些資料

  • Google 帳號基本資料：你用 Google 登入時，本網站會取得你的 Email、名稱和大頭貼網址（Google 的 openid、email、profile 權限）。本網站不會取得你的 Google 密碼，也不會讀取 Gmail、雲端硬碟或其他 Google 服務的內容。
  • 你在本網站產生的讀書資料：每日行程的完成紀錄、休息日設定、考古題進度、小考的答對／答錯紀錄，以及你在題目手寫板上寫的筆跡，和這些資料的最後修改時間。

2. 用途

這些資料只用來讓你登入，並在你的手機、平板、電腦之間同步你的讀書進度和筆跡。本網站不放廣告、不做追蹤或分析、不出售也不分享你的資料給任何人。

3. 存放位置與保護方式

  • 雲端：資料存放在 Supabase 提供的資料庫，傳輸全程加密（HTTPS）。資料表設定了列層級權限（Row Level Security）：每個帳號只能讀寫自己的資料，其他使用者看不到你的資料。
  • 你的裝置：為了沒有網路時也能使用，資料也會存在你瀏覽器的 localStorage 和 IndexedDB。如果同一台裝置換另一個帳號登入，會先清除前一個帳號留在這台裝置上的資料。
  • 網站管理者只會在維護系統或處理刪除要求時存取資料庫。

4. 第三方服務

本網站使用下列服務。它們在提供服務時可能會依各自的隱私權政策處理技術資料（例如 IP 位址）：

  • Google：帳號登入、網頁字型（Google Fonts）
  • Supabase：登入系統與資料庫
  • GitHub Pages：網站主機
  • jsDelivr：載入數學式顯示（MathJax）與 Supabase 程式庫

5. 保存期間與刪除

  • 你的資料會保存到你刪除帳號為止。
  • 隨時可以自己刪除：登入後，在首頁右上角的帳號選單（或教材頁的目錄最下面）按「刪除帳號」。你的帳號、讀書進度、小考紀錄和所有手寫筆跡會從雲端永久刪除，這台裝置上的資料也會一起清除。
  • 你也可以到 Google 帳戶的「第三方應用程式和服務」移除本網站的登入權限。

6. 兒童

本網站是為準備研究所考試的大學生與社會人士設計，不針對 13 歲以下兒童。

7. 政策更新與聯絡方式

政策如有修改，會更新這一頁和上方的生效日期。有任何問題，請到本網站的 GitHub Issues 留言（請不要在留言裡寫 Email 等個人資料）。

Privacy Policy

CS Graduate Exam Planner · Effective November 8, 2026

CS Graduate Exam Planner (“the site”, https://jimmm-lab.github.io/cs-grad-exam-plan/) is a personal, non-commercial study planner and course-notes site for graduate school entrance exams. This policy explains what data the site collects, how it is used, and how you can delete it.

1. Data we collect

  • Basic Google account information: when you sign in with Google, the site receives your email address, name and profile picture URL (the openid, email and profile scopes). The site never receives your Google password and does not access Gmail, Drive or any other Google service.
  • Study data you create on the site: daily plan check-offs, rest-day settings, past-exam progress, quiz results (correct / incorrect), the handwriting you write on the answer pads, and when each item was last changed.

2. How we use it

This data is used only to sign you in and to sync your study progress and handwriting across your phone, tablet and computer. The site shows no ads, does no tracking or analytics, and does not sell or share your data with anyone.

3. Storage and security

  • Cloud: data is stored in a database provided by Supabase and is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Row Level Security ensures each account can read and write only its own data; other users cannot see it.
  • Your device: so the site works offline, data is also kept in your browser’s localStorage and IndexedDB. When a different account signs in on the same device, the previous account’s data on that device is cleared first.
  • The site administrator accesses the database only to maintain the service or to handle deletion requests.

4. Third-party services

The site uses the services below, which may process technical data (such as IP addresses) under their own privacy policies:

  • Google: sign-in and web fonts (Google Fonts)
  • Supabase: authentication and database
  • GitHub Pages: website hosting
  • jsDelivr: loading the math renderer (MathJax) and the Supabase library

5. Retention and deletion

  • Your data is kept until you delete your account.
  • You can delete it yourself at any time: after signing in, open the account menu (top right of the home page, or the bottom of the table of contents on a notes page) and choose “刪除帳號” (Delete account). Your account, study progress, quiz results and all handwriting are permanently removed from the cloud, and the data on that device is cleared as well.
  • You can also remove the site’s access under Third-party apps & services in your Google Account.

6. Children

The site is designed for university students and adults preparing for graduate entrance exams and is not directed at children under 13.

7. Changes and contact

If this policy changes, this page and the effective date above will be updated. For questions, please open an issue on the site’s GitHub Issues page (please do not include your email or other personal information).
